# Provider Icon Source Notes

Self-hosted marks identify providers in the website ticker and dashboard demos.
Product names and marks remain trademarks of their owners.

## CodexBar Baseline

The original CodexBar-backed SVG set is covered by `LICENSE-CodexBar.txt`.
`opencode.svg` and `opencodego.svg` (the OpenCode Zen / OpenCode Go marks) are the OpenCode
first-party mark from `https://opencode.ai/favicon.svg` (the half-filled box), redrawn as the white
glyph the badge/chip expects and mirrored in the app's `provider-opencode.svg` /
`provider-opencodego.svg`. OpenCode Go has no separate mark, so the two share it and are
distinguished by name and tint.
